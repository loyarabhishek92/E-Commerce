
E-commerce (electronic commerce) is the buying and selling of goods, services, or data over the internet, facilitating transactions 24/7 via digital storefronts, websites, and social media. It enables global, scalable, and often lower-cost operations, covering physical goods, digital products, and services for both consumers and businesses.
